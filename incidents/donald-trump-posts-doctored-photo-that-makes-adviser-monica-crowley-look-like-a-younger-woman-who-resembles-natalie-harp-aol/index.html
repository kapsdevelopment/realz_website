Donald Trump Posts Doctored Photo That Makes Adviser Monica Crowley Look Like a Younger Woman Who Resembles Natalie Harp - AOL

President Donald Trump shared a digitally altered image on his Truth Social platform that replaced the face of his adviser Monica Crowley with a younger woman resembling executive assistant Natalie Harp. The original photograph, taken by Getty Images, showed Crowley greeting Chinese President Xi Jinping at Joint Base Andrews, but the posted version also removed First Lady Melania Trump and Chinese First Lady Peng Liyuan from the frame. Additional edits included altering Trump's expression and removing a glove, while superimposing text and stars over the leaders' heads. Media commentators and independent observers noted the significant modifications, with some joking about the resemblance to Natalie Harp.
