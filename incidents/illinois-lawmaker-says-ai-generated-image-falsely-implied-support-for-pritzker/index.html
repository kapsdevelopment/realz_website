Illinois lawmaker says AI-generated image falsely implied support for Pritzker

State Rep. Dave Severin said an AI-generated image circulated on Facebook that combined his photograph and Governor JB Pritzker's, added thumbs-up gestures and an arm around him, and was used to falsely imply Severin endorsed Pritzker in the gubernatorial race. Severin says the true photo was of four people at the Du Quoin State Fair and that the altered image is "100% bogus and non-factual."
