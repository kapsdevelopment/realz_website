Summary

A viral image posted on X on 2026-11-17 purported to be a Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) memo instructing personnel to disable an iOS 27 “Live Listening” feature on government-issued Apple devices. The post showed a document styled as an HSI Chicago memo that warned the update was capturing and sending transcriptions to Apple and provided remediation steps.

Lead Stories reported that four online AI-detection tools (Pangram, Sightengine, ZeroGPT and SynthID) indicated the picture was very likely AI-generated; SynthID flagged the media as “made or edited with OpenAI.” The image was therefore identified as a synthetic, inauthentic document; Lead Stories contacted the Department of Homeland Security and Apple for comment. The post circulated the same day Apple experienced an outage (2026-11-17).
