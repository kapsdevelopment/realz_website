15-year-old allegedly used AI image to frame ex-boyfriend for school shooting threat - Face2Face Africa

Joelle Allen-Robinson, a 15-year-old former Riverview High School student in Hillsborough County, Florida, is accused of using an AI-altered image—a screenshot of an Instagram post—to frame her ex-boyfriend by making it appear he threatened a mass shooting. Deputies say investigators traced the posted image back to Allen-Robinson, who admitted sending the email with the modified image, according to reporting cited by the sheriff’s office. She was arrested and charged with written or electronic threat to conduct a mass shooting or act of terrorism, unlawful use of a two-way communications device, and disrupting a school campus or function; the article cites the relevant Florida statutes and potential felony penalties.
