AI image falsely depicts pro-Palestinian Eiffel Tower protest

An image posted on X on 2026-10-18 purported to show protesters carrying Palestinian flags near the Eiffel Tower in France; the post attracted millions of views and hundreds of thousands of likes. Thai PBS Verify found no matching source via reverse-image search and used three detection tools (Google SynthID, Hive Moderation, and OpenAI’s verification tool), all of which indicated the picture was generated or altered with OpenAI tools and detected a SynthID watermark. X also labeled the image as AI-generated.
