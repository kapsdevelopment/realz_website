Deputy Minister seeks correction over alleged AI-generated image shown on TV - Newswire

Deputy Minister Chathuranga Abeysinghe sent a letter dated October 15 requesting a formal clarification and correction from a private television channel after former Minister Wimal Weerawansa displayed and discussed a photograph the Deputy Minister’s office says was allegedly generated or manipulated using AI. Abeysinghe published what he described as the original selfie showing him with an individual named Amal and said the image shown on the programme had been altered to portray Amal as a Buddhist monk; the Deputy Minister asked the channel to verify the image’s authenticity, correct the broadcast video, issue a public correction and provide a written explanation, warning the alleged use of an AI-generated image could mislead the public and harm reputations or inflame ethnic and religious tensions.
