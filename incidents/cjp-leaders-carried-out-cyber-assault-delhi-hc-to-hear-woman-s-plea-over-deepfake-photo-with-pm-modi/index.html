A woman named Riddhima Sharma filed a plea with the Delhi High Court alleging that four leaders of the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) used AI face-swapping tools to create a deepfake image of her alongside Prime Minister Narendra Modi. The alleged image was displayed during a protest at Jantar Mantar and was described as vulgar and compromising.
