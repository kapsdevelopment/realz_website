Summary

A special POCSO court in Ballia found school transport driver Sanjeet Kumar guilty of using an artificial intelligence application to create an obscene image of a Class 8 student and circulating the altered image on WhatsApp. The victim’s family discovered the fabricated material and the girl’s father filed a police complaint on 2025-05-07.

The court convicted Kumar under Section 6 of the POCSO Act and sentenced him to 25 years of rigorous imprisonment and imposed a fine of Rs 50,000, plus related penal assessments totaling Rs 55,000; non-payment would lead to additional imprisonment. Prosecutors presented digital forensic evidence and witness statements during the trial.
