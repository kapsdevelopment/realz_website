A fake photo allegedly showing a ban on the Ukrainian language in Warsaw taxis is being circulated online — CPD

An AI-generated image depicting a sign in a Warsaw taxi that allegedly told Ukrainians not to speak Ukrainian was circulated online. The Center for Countering Disinformation of the National Security and Defense Council of Ukraine confirmed the image was fake using forgery detection tools. The manipulation was created to discredit Ukrainians in Poland and fuel tensions in Ukrainian-Polish relations.
